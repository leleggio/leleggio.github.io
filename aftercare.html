Aftercare Tips

Aftercare protects your results and helps reduce irritation. These tips are general and may vary by person.

Facials

  • Avoid heavy makeup for 24 hours if possible.
  • Do not exfoliate or use strong acids/retinol for 2–3 days.
  • Use gentle cleanser and moisturizer for the next 48 hours.
  • Wear SPF daily, especially after any exfoliation.
  • Avoid hot showers/saunas and heavy sweating for 24 hours.

Waxing

  • Avoid heat (hot tubs, saunas, long hot showers) for 24–48 hours.
  • Avoid workouts/heavy sweating for 24 hours.
  • Wear loose clothing to reduce friction.
  • Do not pick or scratch the area.
  • Exfoliate gently after 48 hours to help prevent ingrowns.

Brow Lamination and Tint

  • Keep brows dry for 24 hours (no steam, sweating, or water).
  • Avoid oils and heavy skincare on the brow area for 24–48 hours.
  • Do not rub or scrub the brows.
  • Brush brows into place daily using a clean spoolie.
  • Use a brow conditioner if recommended.

Lash Lift and Tint

  • Keep lashes dry for 24 hours (no steam, sweating, or water).
  • Avoid mascara and eye makeup for 24 hours if possible.
  • Do not rub your eyes or sleep with your face pressed into a pillow.
  • Avoid oil-based removers around the eyes.
  • Brush lashes gently with a clean spoolie.
