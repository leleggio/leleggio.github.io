Frequently Asked Questions

1. What should I do before my appointment?

Arrive with clean skin if possible and avoid heavy exfoliation the day before.

2. How do I know what service to book?

If you are unsure, use the Contact Form linked in the footer and tell me your goals.

3. How early should I arrive?

Arrive 5–10 minutes early so we can start on time.

4. What if I have sensitive skin?

Let me know before the service. We can adjust products and steps for comfort.

5. How often should I get facials?

Most people benefit from every 4–6 weeks, depending on goals and skin type.

6. What should I avoid after a facial?

Avoid exfoliating products, strong acids/retinol, and heavy sweating for 24–48 hours.

7. How do I prevent ingrown hairs after waxing?

After 48 hours, gently exfoliate and keep the skin moisturized.

8. How long do brow and lash services last?

Most lash lifts last about 6–8 weeks, and brow services vary based on hair growth.
